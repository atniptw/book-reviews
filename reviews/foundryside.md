# Foundryside (Books 1–3)

**Author:** Robert Jackson Bennett

**Rating:** 4/5

**Review Date:** 2026-08-18

**Format:** Audiobook

**Reading Context:** Mostly before bed

---

## Review

Robert Jackson Bennett has a pattern here: an incredible opening book that the sequels don't quite live up to. Foundryside is genuinely one of the most fun reads I've had recently — great heist setup, great world-building, great characters. Shorefall is solid but feels more like a bridge to book three than its own story, and Locklands reaches for a huge, time-jumping, world-ending finale that didn't feel earned — Berenice being left alone for decades at the end didn't sit right with me, even with the reunion epilogue. It's a contrast to his other series, Shadow of the Leviathan (Tainted Cup, A Drop of Corruption), which hasn't shown that same falloff — at least not yet.

## What I Liked

- Foundryside's heist premise and world-building
- Sancia and Berenice's relationship feels genuine
- Audiobook narration throughout

## What I Disliked

- Shorefall feels more like a bridge to book 3 than its own story
- Locklands' time-jump/world-ending finale didn't feel earned
- Berenice left alone for decades at the end didn't sit right
- Clear falloff from a great start, book to book

## Additional Notes

Worth it for Foundryside alone even if the back half tapers off. If you liked the world-building in Shadow of the Leviathan, this is the same strength paired with a shakier finish. Rated as one entry at 4/5; individually more like 5/4/3, book to book.
