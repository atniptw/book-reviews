# He Who Fights with Monsters (Books 1–12)

**Author:** Shirtaloon, Travis Deverell

**Rating:** 4/5

**Review Date:** 2026-08-18

**Format:** Audiobook

**Reading Context:** While running, though I eventually started binging it

---

## Review

Twelve books in, this is still a series I enjoy, even if it's not as sharp as when it started. The best stretch by far is the opening arc — getting pulled into the new world — and everything since has been a mixed bag, with some arcs landing better than others (call it a 4.5 overall, but some entries are more like a 3, others a 5). Jason is clearly written as someone carrying real trauma, but the books keep treating him like he's just being a stubborn kid instead of actually reckoning with that, and it got more noticeable to me the longer the series went. I listened to the whole thing as an audiobook and the narration held up across all twelve books, but I'm still not sold on where his powers have gone lately — the tree soul, him making rabbit people — that stuff hasn't clicked for me yet.

## What I Liked

- The opening arc (new world) is still the high point of the series
- Still funny twelve books in
- Skill/character progression stayed engaging across arcs
- Audiobook narration held up over the long haul

## What I Disliked

- Jason's trauma gets played as childish stubbornness instead of something the story reckons with
- Character-sheet info-dumping swung from too much early on to almost none by book 12 — never found a middle ground
- Romance beats feel a bit unnatural
- Recent power developments (tree soul, rabbit people) haven't won me over

## Additional Notes

Great pick for LitRPG/progression fantasy fans who can enjoy a long ride without needing every arc to land equally well — the opening arc alone is worth it. Rated as one series entry; individual books range higher and lower than the 4/5 here.
