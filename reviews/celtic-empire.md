# Celtic Empire

**Author:** Clive Cussler and Dirk Cussler

**Rating:** 4/5

**Review Date:** 2026-06-22

**Format:** Audiobook

**Reading Context:** Before bed

---

## Review

A decent entry in the Dirk Pitt series — I liked the story, and the history kept me engaged even knowing a lot of it is invented. The globe-trotting adventure holds up; it's the Pitt-kids subplot that drags it down a notch for me. Solid before-bed listening.

## What I Liked

- The story and pacing
- The historical thread, made-up or not

## What I Disliked

- Pitt's kids surviving on luck, not smarts
- They know bad people are after them but never check if they're being followed

## Additional Notes

Perfect for fans of long-running Dirk Pitt adventure thrillers who can roll with a contrived subplot or two.
